# GameTime Support

Help for the iPhone app listed on the App Store as "GameTime: Game Session Planner" (called GameTime below).

## Contact

Email me at coledev67@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. I usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I add a friend?**
Share your invite link or QR code from your profile. When someone accepts it, you become friends and can see each other's profile and sessions. Anyone with your invite link can become your friend, so share it carefully.

**How do I report or block someone?**
Every profile and session has a Report option. You can block anyone from their profile or from Settings > Blocked. Blocked people can't send you invites or see your sessions. The [Terms of Use](TERMS.md) explain how reports are handled.

**How do I restore the Supporter Pack?**
Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. Refunds are handled by Apple through [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Where is my data kept?**
In your own iCloud, under your Apple Account. I can't read it. The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) explains what's stored and who can see it.

**Sessions or invites aren't showing up.**
GameTime depends on iCloud. Check that you're signed in to iCloud, that iCloud Drive and GameTime are turned on in Settings > [your name] > iCloud, and that you have a connection. Then pull to refresh or reopen the app.
